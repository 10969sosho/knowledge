## Sections
1. **Loading Screen** - Animated intro (logo + loading bar, 1.5s)
2. **Navbar** - Transparent→solid on scroll, mobile hamburger, active link
3. **Hero** - Full-screen bg image, animated stats: 20+ countries, 300+ containers, 1000+ briket tons, 24/7 support, 5+ years
4. **About** - Two-column with experience badge + checklist
5. **Why Choose Us** - 4 cards: Premium Quality, Custom Specifications, Reliable Shipping, Certified Quality
6. **Products** - Shisha Charcoal, BBQ Briquette, Coconut Fiber, Coconut Spices, Cinnamon (with Request Quote buttons)
7. **Certifications** - Document certification section
8. **Gallery** - Factory & warehouse images
9. **Testimonials** - Auto-rotating carousel
10. **Contact** - Form (name, email, subject, message) + WhatsApp

## Interactive Features
