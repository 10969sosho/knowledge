# Otherlands Homepage

## Overview

Single-page cinematic production house homepage implemented from the Otherlands brief. The implementation lives at `/Users/10969sosho/PROJECT/CVSS/WEBSITE RINGAN/WEBSITE AGENCY`.

- Editorial selected-works index, in-depth rows, client statement, trusted-by grid, and contact footer.
- Remote Unsplash photography and Google Fonts are prototype assets.
